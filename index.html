Materi Pokok

What is a report text?

Report text adalah teks yang menyampaikan informasi tentang sesuatu apa adanya, berdasarkan pengamatan dan penelitian, misalnya tentang hewan, tumbuhan, tempat, atau benda.

Social function: to present factual information about a subject in general, not about one particular thing.

Contoh: teks tentang bekantan pada umumnya adalah report text; cerita tentang bekantan bernama Beki bukan.

Generic structure

1. General classificationMemperkenalkan subjek dan mengelompokkannya (jenis, famili, asal).
2. DescriptionMerinci ciri: bagian tubuh, ukuran, warna, makanan, kebiasaan, habitat. Dapat terdiri dari beberapa paragraf.

Pada materi ini deskripsi dipisah menjadi Description (ciri fisik) dan Habitat & behavior (tempat hidup dan perilaku).

Model text

The Proboscis Monkey

The proboscis monkey (Nasalis larvatus), known in Indonesia as bekantan, is a primate of the family Cercopithecidae. It is endemic to the island of Borneo and is the mascot of South Kalimantan.

An adult male has a very large, fleshy nose that can hang over its mouth, while the female's nose is smaller and pointed. Its fur is reddish-brown on the back and light grey on the limbs and tail. Males can weigh up to 24 kilograms, almost twice as heavy as females. The round belly holds a large stomach for digesting leaves.

Proboscis monkeys live in mangrove forests, swamps, and along riverbanks. They are excellent swimmers because their feet are partly webbed. They eat mainly leaves, unripe fruits, and seeds, and live in groups led by one dominant male. Because of habitat loss, the species is listed as Endangered.
